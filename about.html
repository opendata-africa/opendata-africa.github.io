About Open Data Africa

Empowering African AI research through open, accessible, and high-quality datasets

Our Mission

Open Data Africa is a comprehensive portal dedicated to aggregating, curating, and providing access to high-quality open datasets across the African continent. We believe that accessible data is the foundation of innovation, evidence-based policy, and sustainable development.

Our platform serves researchers, policymakers, developers, and organizations working to solve Africa's most pressing challenges through AI and data-driven solutions.

Our Methodology

1

Curation & Verification

Every dataset is manually reviewed for quality, licensing compliance, and relevance to African contexts.

2

Metadata Standardization

We use comprehensive metadata including 19 fields covering provenance, licensing, temporal coverage, and use cases.

3

Open Access Commitment

All datasets feature open licenses (CC-BY, CC0, MIT, Apache) ensuring free access for research and development.

4

Community-Driven

We collaborate with African research communities (Masakhane, Lacuna Fund, Deep Learning Indaba) to identify and curate datasets.
